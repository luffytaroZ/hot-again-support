Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

What Hot Again stores

Hot Again stores your goals, check-ins, journal notes, routine progress, and settings on your device. We do not sell your personal data.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional. If you give permission, Hot Again can read sleep, steps, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, and mindful minutes. This health data is used on your device to make your daily view and wellness insights. Hot Again does not write to Apple Health. We do not send Apple Health data to RevenueCat or another server.

Subscriptions

Hot Again uses Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat to check if your paid access is active. Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase details and a customer ID. They do not receive your Apple Health data from Hot Again.

Tracking

Hot Again does not use ad tracking and does not show ads.

Your choices

You can turn Apple Health access off in iPhone Settings. You can reset your local app data by deleting the app. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings.
